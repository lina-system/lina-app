# Work cards

Each piece of work is one card: a work card (a kanban sub-card an agent runs a slice of a goal in). A worker run is never a card of its own in a list: it shows inside its card, goal or to-do (lina-app#141). The home (진행 중인 작업) lists the cards in one order, and each card updates in place from the change stream. A card is a kind icon, the agent mark, a title and one grey state-and-time line; a work card's title is the core's checked line when it is written, else the card's own title until then, while a run's own page without a checked title shows its kind word in grey. Every other line is the core's checked line (`plain`, lina#151), never a file name, a run id or an English topic.

## Platforms

- `web`: shipped.
- `mac`: planned (M1).
- `iphone`: planned (M1).

## Intended behavior

- Home order (lina-app#141, #152), the same at both widths: one `결정 필요 <n>` line (only when there are any, the inbox's number) and the inbox's `검토 필요` group; the running cards under `진행 중` with their count (with none, the head alone reads `진행 중 0` when anything is stopped or planned); the owner's to-dos under the head `내 할 일 <n>` (every to-do counted, `할 일 추가` beside it), in one order (lina-app#198): dated ones by their date, the earliest first (overdue, then today, then later dates), then the undated ones, and of the same date (or both undated) the newest record first, whether or not it waits, so no dated to-do sits under an undated one; their first three rows (the first three in that order) open, then one fold `외 n개` that opens the rest in place; the stopped cards in their own `중단됨` group with one red count of every stopped card (`중단됨 · <reason>` in red; running.md, lina-app#134), their first five rows, then one fold `외 n개`; the planned cards under `예정` with their count (lina-app#148); `대기` (the waiting cards `검토 필요` does not already hold); then `완료`: its head counts its rows (a `×N` line is one row) and the finished cards not read yet, the newest five read first and one fold `외 n개` for the rest, which opens them in place, its line saying `불러오는 중` meanwhile, so no row above it moves (lina-app#186, #221). Every opened fold (`내 할 일`, `중단됨`, `완료`) ends in `<group> 닫기`, which shows the first rows and the fold again and puts the list back at the scroll it had when the fold was opened (lina-app#221). On a phone the first screen shows the decisions and the first stopped rows before any to-do, however many to-dos the owner holds. A to-do's and a running card's title take a second line before they are cut, like a stopped row's. No line counts the work nothing names (running.md).
- A finished card says when it ended (`완료 · 20분 전`, `완료 · 2일 전`); how long it took (`소요 20분`) is in its detail. Under `완료` the cards that ended in the last 24 hours come first, then the older ones. A work card's end and a stopped card's last beat come from its list row (`endedAt`, `lastHeartbeatAt`, lina#577), so they are right before the card is ever opened. A core whose rows carry neither leaves an unopened finished card at `완료` with no time, after the timed ones, and a stopped one timed from its elapsed time, until it opens (lina-app#100).
- The first load reads the lists only, never each card's detail: every list area reads `불러오는 중` until the lists answer, then its rows (or `없음`). A list whose reads all failed before any answered is not known, not empty (lina-app#199): no `0`, no `없음`, and no `내 할 일` head or `할 일 추가` over the to-dos; the head says `읽기 실패` (freshness.md) until a read answers. A page opened again draws the rows it kept from the last visit at once, with no `불러오는 중`, and reads only what changed since (live.md, lina-app#194).
- A wide window opens on the home, and so does the phone: the 작업 tab's first screen is the home under the tabs, the pressed `작업` its one title (lina-app#193), its lines readable without a tap. The menu (오늘, 수신함, 진행 중인 작업, 마감일, 완료, the agents, 목표) opens from the menu icon `메뉴` at the left of the phone's bar as a bottom sheet; choosing a row goes there and closes it (lina-app#124).
- No add button is drawn, on either width, while no route writes a new to-do (lina-app#95 brings it back); a control is never drawn disabled without a reason.
- One card per run: follow-up rounds of one card stay one card. Cards are never merged, with one exception for lines (lina-app#168): in `완료` on the home and on the 완료 screen, cards whose titles read the same (titles that differ only by a hash or a number the screen cuts, and the cards a goal's title names first, lina-app#237) are one line with their count after the title (`×3`) and the newest one's time, and choosing it opens the newest card. That card's detail lists every card of the line under `묶인 작업 N건` (lina-app#171): one `<time> · <state word> · <sentence>` line each (the sentence is its result line, else the first readable sentence of its latest note, else its own name), the opened card first, then newest first, each the link to its own card; opened from one of those links, a card of the line still lists them all, itself first. Running, stopped, planned, waiting cards and the owner's to-dos never collapse. A goal's state is one fold of its cards everywhere (its page from 작업 and from 대화, the goal lists, lina-app#168): a done goal stays done, a stopped card makes it `중단됨`, a running card `진행 중`, else the record's state; its cards are the ones its detail links and the ones whose runs take on the goal or one of its to-dos. No worker run is a row in the finished groups, 완료 or the agent screen (lina-app#141): runs started by a card show only inside its detail, and a run's page still opens from its address.
- A row's two lines (lina-app#152): line 1 is its goal's title (the card's own `goalTitle` first), or the card's own title when it has no goal; line 2 is the card's own title whenever it differs from line 1. One title in a list is one line, or its lines differ in line 1 (lina-app#242): when several unfinished rows of one list share line 1 (one goal over several cards), each with an own title leads with it and the goal's title is its line 2, and its detail head reads that own title with `목표 · <goal>` under it; 완료 folds rows of one title into one `×n` line instead. Neither line is ever cut: each wraps at a word and the row grows, at 390 too (lina-app#156). Line 2 is never a bare time: two rows of a group that still read the same say when they last moved as a clock time in their time slot. Every row and detail title loses its card and goal ids (`t93`, `G106`, `T7`, `OT-12`, `t_586c9120`), issue references (`#12`, `lina#49`, `lina-app #131`), hashes and bots' time stamps (`[이름 16:52]`), each with the particle glued to it (`<hash>로`, `#12를`), and a bracket left with nothing readable, or with only a word that leaned on its refs (`(lina#49 뒷일)`, `(#12 관련 건)`, lina-app#246), goes whole, while one that keeps a clause of its own stays; the rest of the sentence stays, its double spaces and empty brackets collapsed (lina-app#156). A cut that leaves a hole gives no title at all (lina-app#218), among them a bracket of words that was an aside on a ref or a Latin word cut from right after a separator or the title's start, with more of the title after it, which would open the clause: `손보기: lina#12(지난 판) 이후` never reads `손보기: (지난 판) 이후` (lina-app#228). A finished card's title is drawn only when the one rule every drawn sentence passes lets it (lina-app#222, `sentenceMayShow`): no Hangul, a Latin word (an English word, a product or tool name, `PR`, `QA`, `OCX`, a round label), a start inside a sentence, an open end or a hole hides it, the checked title too; the row reads its next name (its goal's title, its checked or own title, its to-do's). A stopped, planned or running card's titles (its goal's, its checked and its own) are read by the row title rule instead (lina-app#156, #225, `workTitle` in `src/model/index.ts`): only what is internal goes, and a title is hidden only with no Hangul left, a run of three Latin words or more, or raw text such as a path or a command; product and tool names and short abbreviations (`MV`, `CLI`) stay. A record goal's or to-do's title (the goal list, a decision's context) the rule refuses reads by its Hangul words, as a finished card's does (`foldTitle`); 마감일 and 내 할 일 rows draw the record's own title. A finished card's titles (its goal's, its own, its to-do's) are drawn exactly as written or not at all (lina-app#231, `wholeTitle` in `src/model/index.ts`): the only thing taken out is a bracket that holds nothing but refs, ids or hashes (`(lina#12·#13)`, `(a1b2c3d)`); a title that would need any other cut (a Latin word, a ref or a hash outside such a bracket, the debris they leave: `… (추가)`, a particle left at the start, a dangling `·`, `… (포함)로`, an `@` token, a number left in a bracket) is not drawn, and a finished card none of its titles names is counted in 완료's grey line. A stopped, running or planned row whose own title is hidden reads by its Hangul words, its Latin words cut with the particle glued after them, after its ask (lina-app#214, #225). A hidden own title leaves the goal's title; a goal-less card with none is named by the core's `ask` (a stopped card's), then those Hangul words, else its kind of work in grey: never `제목 없음`, never by whose card it is, and never left out of its group, so each group's head is its rows (lina-app#225). A row, its detail head's row and its goal read one state, the card's kanban status: a worker run the hub serves under the card's own id never stands in for the card.
- The title is `plain.title`; a running card's now line (wide windows) is `plain.now`. A work card without a checked title shows its own title, shortened to the row width (never sliced), on the home, 완료, the agent screen and its detail head, and its kind is read from the same text — but only while that own title passes the title rule above (no Hangul, or an English sentence, fails it). An own title the rule refuses draws nothing: the card keeps its kind of work in grey (`코드 작업`, `문서 작업`, `조사`, `기기 작업`), and its kind is read from the checked title alone. A run without a checked title keeps its kind of work in grey. A line the core has not written, or one that failed its check, is null and never shows raw text; a missing now line draws nothing.
- A card belongs to the record goal its `goalTitle` (or its work goal's title) names, as its row's line 1 says, and to the goal and to-do its runs take on (lina-app#168, #170): the goal's page and its rows in the goal lists, and each to-do's state, fold those cards (goals.md), so no screen reads a goal or a to-do differently from the cards the work tab shows.
- When the writer of the checked lines has stopped, the header says nothing of it: the owner cannot act on it, so it is one row `요약 중단됨` in `상태`'s `점검` panel (lina-app#150).
- Every count equals its list (lina-app#221, #225): the sidebar's `진행 중인 작업` grey number and red `중단됨 n` are the running list, and each other section head is its rows. 완료 counts cards, one unit in every state (lina-app#225): the sidebar's `완료` is the work tab's `완료` head, every finished card the core holds (`finishedTotal`, lina#729) while the page has read only the newest five (the kept copy holds those newest five too, whatever order the list sends them in, so the preview is newest first in every state, lina-app#231), and the cards the read of every finished card sent once it has answered; a `×N` line stands for N cards and says N on its row, so the rows' `×N` add up to the head opened. Its fold `외 n개` counts the lines it opens (lina-app#237): the group reads every finished card right after the page's first five, the fold reading `불러오는 중` with no number until they are in, so the lines drawn and its n are the lines opened, on the first load and later. Opening the fold never changes the number (the hub's 525 finished cards read 525 collapsed and opened, in 476 rows); it moves only when the read lands with another count than the total, which the core counts apart from its list, so cards that finish during the read are another moment's (lina-app#217). The 완료 screen lists those same lines and counts the same cards (lina-app#179). The finished cards nothing names are one grey line `제목 없는 n건` (done.md, lina-app#231): it says what it holds and how many, never a bare kind word, and counts its n cards as a `×N` line does; never `제목 없음`. An old core that sends no `finishedTotal` sends every card, and the count is those cards.
- A card's detail: its kind icon and word, its name and state line; then `지금` (`plain.now`), `완료된 것` (only its result line, when it has one), `할 일` (the to-dos it owns), `기록` (its rounds, newest first, each head one line `<time> · <outcome>[ · <result sentence>]`, no round number, linking to the round's worker run; see card-detail.md) and its comments. The rounds show once: the worker runs' box, also headed `기록` (its worker runs by round, in the secondary label colour and regular weight) stands only while the record is not shown, and under the error line when the record's read failed. A box shows only when it adds something: nothing repeats the to-do list.
- A running card with to-dos shows a thin bar and its count (`1/3`) after its lines on a wide window; on a phone the bar is in its detail head only.
- A comment on a card is one history line, `<time> · <sentence>`, its author the line's tooltip (lina-app#142), newest first (lina-app#149). The owner's comment reads exactly as saved; a bot's is the whole comment or none, by the one rule (card-detail.md, lina-app#226, #236): a comment with a sentence holding a path, a command, a hash, an issue number, an address or an id (`G73`, `t_27363156`, `rc=1`), an English word, the hub's own cut (`…`), or that starts inside a word or a number, is not drawn at all, never cut down to what is left or shortened. A machine notice (by the dispatcher or the worker, or opening with a tag such as `[받음]`, `[대기 충족]`) is no line; a run of comments with no sentence drawn is one `접힌 댓글` line (`접힌 댓글 n건`).
- A round whose start fell out of the kept history shows one dim line, `시작 기록 없음`, only when it holds content (a line it said, or a worker run), and draws that content under it dimmed; a round with only its end on record shows nothing. A line that would show a path or a command reads `접힌 댓글`.
- A stopped card's detail head shows `중단됨 · <reason>` in red, as on the home; its time stays grey.
- One word per wait (lina-app#174, `waitWord` in `src/words.ts`): a stopped card's row and a to-do's or goal's record read the same word for the same reason, `결정 대기`, `피드백 대기`, `다른 일 대기` (another to-do first). On a record `waiting` the word stands alone (`피드백 대기 · 2일 전`), never `대기 · 피드백` or `대기 · 진행 대기`; after another state it follows it (`중단됨 · 피드백 대기`). No line says `대기` twice; a `hold` record reads `대기 · 보류`.
- A card just created (예정, not started) shows on its agent's screen, in a `예정 n` group after `검토 필요`, with no time beside it (agent-screen.md), and on the home in its own `예정` group under `진행 중`, so a card that turns planned never leaves the tab (lina-app#148).
- 완료's agent filter is the address (`#/done?agent=<id>`): it survives a reload and every live update, nothing goes to web storage, each choice's count is the list it shows, and the filtered list's `최근 활동 <time>` reads the same as on the agent's screen.
- A card's detail and a worker run's page show the run's own timeline below the boxes and above the comments (card-detail.md); it refetches on the run's `worker` change event and keeps its open state.
- A card's checked lines change in place when its kanban event arrives: only the card list and that card's detail are read again (live.md).

## Sub-features

- `cards-home` orders the home: decisions, review, running, the owner's to-dos (their first three), stopped (their first five), planned, waiting, the last 24 hours, older (lina-app#152)
- `cards-lines` draws a row's line 1 as its goal's title (else the card's own), line 2 as the card's own title when it differs, never a bare time and never a card or goal id (lina-app#152); rows of one list that share a goal lead with their own titles (lina-app#242)
- `cards-one` keeps one card per run and per job, and no worker run as a row
- `cards-plain` draws a work card's checked title, or its own title until that exists when the raw-text rule lets it; its detail head never reads a kind word (lina-app#149), and a missing now line nothing
- `cards-writer` says 요약 중단됨 in 상태 · 점검 when the writer stopped, never beside the header
- `cards-counts` makes every count equal its list
- `cards-detail` shows now, done so far, next, to-dos and worker runs by round
- `cards-progress` draws a running card's to-dos as a bar and a count
- `cards-folded` draws a bot's comment whole, or folds it when any of it holds a path, a command or an id or it is longer than a whole line
- `cards-no-start` draws a round with no start on record as one dim line, only with content
- `cards-done-filter` keeps 완료's agent filter in the address
- `cards-planned` shows a card just created on its agent's screen and in the home's `예정` group
- `cards-timeline` shows each run's rounds once, newest first, heads linking to their worker runs, sentences bright and tools dim

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the app on a wide window, or choose `진행 중인 작업` on a phone's first screen.
- Choose a card for its detail.
- Address `/app/#/running` or `/app/#/cards/<id>`.
