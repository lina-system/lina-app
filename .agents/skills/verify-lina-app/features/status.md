# 기기·쿼터

기기·쿼터 shows each node with its state, reason, usage and load, and under it the runners it can run now: the version and a signed-in mark, or one reason. A runner that needs sign-in has one button. A node row can restart its worker or OCX. The owner's own Macs follow in `내 기기`, with status and tool versions only. When the core serves the hub's power equipment, one `전원` card follows, read only. Then the quota per service with its burn rate, reset time and each account's state, what the models cost in tokens and dollars for today, 7 days and 30 days, OCX's own row, and the hub's own checks in a `점검` panel. The screen reads the core (`/api/lina/devices`, `/api/lina/quota`, `/api/lina/usage?range=…`, `/api/lina/models/state` and `/api/lina/status/checks`) on the same refresh trigger as the rest, at most once a minute while the page is visible, and reads devices again right after a restart or a sign-in, so the screen matches the core within one minute. A sign-in that is still running stays `로그인 중` until a later devices read.

## Platforms

- `web`: shipped.
- `mac`: planned (M1).
- `iphone`: planned (M1).

## Intended behavior

- The machines line (`상태`'s sidebar on a wide window, above the machines on the phone's `상태`) adds up: `기기 n · 정상 n · 경고 n · 꺼짐 n`. `경고` (orange) and `꺼짐` (red) show only when not 0. It counts nodes only, work then the file node, never the owner's own Macs. On the screen each block's head counts what that block lists, so the two agree: `작업 기기 n · 정상 n` (with `경고 n` and `꺼짐 n` when not 0) over the nodes, `내 기기 n` with `연결됨 n` and `연결 끊김 n` (each when not 0) over the owner's Macs.
- One row per node, work nodes first and the file node last: name, state word `정상` / `경고` (orange) / `꺼짐` (red), the one reason word (`연결 끊김`, `디스크`, `메모리`, `CPU`, `온도`, `실행기`, `작업 초과`, `응답 없음`), thin CPU, 메모리 and 디스크 bars for the readings the core has, and for the ones it lacks one reason from the core's `metricsWhy`, once per row and never a dash: `측정 원본 없음` (`no_source`), `측정 기록 없음` (`unknown`, or a value the app does not know) and `기기 응답 없음` (`down`); then `작업 n/상한` (`작업 —/상한` when unknown). A row that is fine shows nothing extra. A node with `reachable: false` reads its name, `꺼짐 · 연결 끊김` and nothing else: no readings, load, runners or restart.
- Under each node, a grid of its runners. The runner row starts at the same left edge as the node's name, past the status dot, at 1280 and at 390, in the name's weight. A runner is fine when the core reports `ready: true`, whatever its `signIn` (the hub serves `signed_in`, `magpie` for omo and claude, and `unknown`); only when `ready` is null does a signed-in value (`signed_in`, `api_key`, `ocx`) with no reason make it fine. A fine runner shows its installed version and a signed-in mark (`로그인됨`). Any other cell shows one reason word: `업데이트 필요` followed by the installed and the fleet version (`5.1.60 → 5.1.69`, the version number only, when both are known and differ), `준비 필요`, `로그인 필요` or `연결 끊김`. A runner with `ready: false` and no reason reads `준비 필요`. No cell reads `확인 중`, a state that never ends: a runner with no sign-in answer, the core's own `unknown` reason or a reason the app does not know shows its installed version alone, or only its name when there is none.
- A `로그인 필요` cell has one `로그인` button. While the post is in flight, and while the core reports that runner `signingIn`, the cell reads `로그인 중` and shows no button. When `signingIn` turns false, a signed-in runner shows the mark; otherwise the reason and the `로그인` button return. A refusal shows one Korean line by status in the warning colour, in the cell, never the core's English detail: `이 기기에서는 볼 수만 있습니다` (403), `쓰기가 너무 많음` (429), `기기를 찾을 수 없음` (404), `이미 진행 중` (409), `아직 지원하지 않음` (501), `기기가 거절함` (502), or `보내지 못함`.
- A node row has one `서비스 다시 시작` menu (named `<node> 서비스 다시 시작`), with `작업 실행기` and `모델 연결`; a node that is `꺼짐` or unreachable has none. Each asks once to confirm before it posts. Taken, the row shows nothing new. Refused, the same status line shows on the row in the warning colour. On a phone the menu is a bottom sheet.
- After the nodes, `내 기기` lists the owner's own Macs: the name, `연결됨` or `연결 끊김` (or `확인 중` before the first read), and each tool once with its installed version (the name alone when the Mac read none, never a dash). A Mac with `reachable: false` reads `연결 끊김` and nothing else, no tools. No buttons and no runner reasons. The group is absent when there are none.
- `전원` sits between `내 기기` and `쿼터`, one card, only when the devices read carries a `power` section; no section, no card. Its first line is how old the watch's record is, `갱신 방금` / `갱신 n분 전`; a stopped watch reads `감시 끊김 · n분 전` and a failed cloud read `클라우드 읽기 실패 · n분 전`, both orange; no record reads `전원 기록 없음` (or `전원 기록 읽기 오류`) with no numbers at all, never zeros. Then one row per power station: a state dot (red when its link is down, orange when an alert names it), the name, a battery bar, and `<n>% · 입력 <n>W · 출력 <n>W · <n>°C · <charge>`, the charge `충전 중`, `배터리 사용`, `완충` or `대기`, with `<n>시간 후 방전` only while on battery; a station without a battery reads only `연결됨` or `연결 끊김`. A row whose value is too old to trust (the core's `stale`: older than 15 minutes, or a stopped watch or a failed cloud read) keeps its numbers, dimmed, and shows its age in the trail (`—` when unknown). One `허브` line follows: `벽 전원 연결됨` / `벽 전원 끊김`, `배터리 n%` and the uplink `유선` or `핫스팟`, dimmed when the watch stopped. The active alerts close the card as one red line, `경고: <alert> · <alert>`, each in words (`정전`, `<station> 온도`, `<station> 연결 끊김`, `클라우드 읽기 실패`, `<station> 실시간 연결 끊김`, else `기타 경고`). No button, link or form.
- One row per quota service, named as the core gives it except Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok for the known ones: percent bar, percent, `시간당 +n%` (its title reads `최근 5시간 평균`), the reset as `n시간 후` under a day and `n일 후` at or over a day, and a red `주의` when an account warns: the core's `warn`, set when the rate reaches 100% before the reset. A service without a rate shows only its percent and reset.
- A service row unfolds to its accounts. An account reads `계정 n`, then the same convention the service row uses: the percent used of its main window, the rate and the reset. An account OCX needs signed in again says only `계정 n · 다시 로그인 필요` in the warning colour, with no percent, rate, reset or bar, because none of its numbers is current; a paused account shows `계정 n · 일시 중지` and keeps its reset, without the rate. Accounts are folded by default.
- The last rows of the same 쿼터 list are not services, and take the same row look and inset: one row for each service OCX holds no account for, `<service> · 계정 없음`, then OCX's own row `모델 <enabled>개 켜짐` (the proxy is never named), followed by ` · 연결 끊김: <machine>, <machine>` only when a link is not fine. The machines it names are the ones the `기기` panel names, and the link that is down is that panel's 꺼짐 machine, so one machine never carries two names on one screen.
- The `사용량` panel sits between `쿼터` and `점검`: a segmented control `오늘` / `7일` / `30일`, `오늘` chosen at first; the chosen range's totals as `$<cost> · 토큰 <n>`; then the two short lists `모델별` and `기기별`, each the five costliest rows as `<name> · $<cost> · 토큰 <n>`. The cost keeps two decimals under $100 and none from $100 up; tokens read `1.2만` from ten thousand and `3.4억` from a hundred million. The core serves no split by agent, so the panel says nothing about agents.
- The `점검` panel sits under the `사용량` panel and shows the hub's checks: a failing, stale or unreadable check takes one open row, `<name> · <line>`, with `실패` (red), `갱신 지연` (orange) or `읽기 오류` (red) and how long ago as `n분 전` / `n시간 전` / `n일 전`, the state word over the time at the row's right edge, inset like the `기기` and `쿼터` rows; a check that passes says nothing. Passing checks fold into one line `정상 n`, so a panel whose checks all pass shows only that, and a panel with no checks reads `점검 없음`. The sidebar line still counts machines only. A check whose line is not Korean (an engine's English error) reads one plain Korean line by the check's id and the line's `ok:` / `hold:` / `fail:` (`runner-sync` reads `실행기 업데이트 완료` / `보류` / `실패`); an id the app does not know reads `점검 실패` or `점검 보류` (after the name when the name is Korean). The original line folds under `자세히` in the row, wrapped, never cut by the width.

## Sub-features

- `status-line` sums the machines in the sidebar
- `status-machines` lists machines with state and load
- `status-meters` draws CPU, memory and disk as thin bars
- `status-quota` lists quota per service with rate, reset and warning, then one row per service with no account and OCX's own row
- `status-accounts` unfolds a service to its accounts, each with the percent used, a bare `다시 로그인 필요`, or `일시 중지` and its reset
- `status-usage` totals tokens and cost for the chosen range and lists the costliest models and machines
- `status-ocx` gives OCX the last row of the quota list: how many models are on, and which machines' links are down
- `status-checks` keeps failing, stale and unreadable checks open and folds passing ones into `정상 n`
- `status-runners` shows each node's runners as a version and a signed-in mark when the core says ready, or one reason word, starting under the node's name
- `status-sign-in` starts sign-in from the one button and shows the mark when the node reports it
- `status-restart` restarts the node's worker or OCX after one confirm
- `status-person` lists the owner's own Macs with status and tool versions, and does not count them
- `status-power` shows the hub's power equipment as one read-only card: each station's level, in and out, temperature and charge, stale values dimmed with their age, the hub's line and the active alerts

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose `상태` in the tabs: a phone opens on this screen, a wide window on its `기기` line under `기기·쿼터`.
- Address `/app/#/status`.

## Gotchas

- With `&error=quota` the quota list is gone and the toolbar reads `읽기 실패: 쿼터`; that variant belongs to freshness.
- With `&error=checks` the checks list is gone, the panel reads `점검 없음` and the toolbar reads `읽기 실패: 점검`; the machines, quota, OCX's row and usage still draw. That variant belongs to freshness.
- The usage numbers are the core's own estimates: a row OCX priced none of sorts after the priced ones and reads `—` for its cost.
- OCX cannot read an account that needs signing in again, so that account's row carries no numbers at all; a service whose every account is signed out or paused would show a row with only its reset, which the scene avoids.
- Hours round up and days round to the nearest day, so the scene's readings hold for the whole drive.
- The minute-long refresh of machines, quota, usage, OCX's models and links and the checks is held by a unit test (`src/data/data.test.ts`); a drive is too short to see it. After a restart the screen reads devices once at once. After a sign-in it reads devices at once (that read shows `signingIn`) and once more, which on this scene is the signed-in mark. A sign-in the core is still running stays `로그인 중` until a later devices read says it ended.
- The power card reads only what the core's devices route serves (lina-system/lina#483); the app has no route to the power devices, and the card has nothing to press. `&power=stopped` and `&power=none` are the synthetic scene's variants for a stopped watch and a core that serves no section.
- The hub serves `metricsWhy` on each node, but the client schema and the synthetic scene do not carry it yet, so on this drive the reachable nodes all have readings and no reason shows. The reasons, the folded check original and the runner versions on the hub's real shapes are held by `src/screens/status.test.tsx`.
- Sign-in on this drive is the synthetic scene. The real-device check of the button follows lina-system/lina#230 and is not part of this screen's proof.
