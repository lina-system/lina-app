# 작업 detail

A work card (`#/cards/<id>`) or a worker run outside any card (`#/runs/<id>`) opens its detail in the one detail frame: title, state and time, what it is doing now, the to-dos it covers, the worker runs it started (dimmed, one line each) and its comments, one line each.

## Platforms

- `web`: shipped.
- `mac`: planned (M1).
- `iphone`: planned (M1).

## Intended behavior

- A card or a run opens in the one detail frame (lina-app#142), wherever it is chosen: the right-hand pane beside the list at desktop width (it scrolls on its own, so it opens where the owner is looking however far down the list they chose it; the window, the toolbar and the menu never scroll away), a sheet rising from the bottom over the list at phone width, with `닫기`. The tab, the header and the list stay as they were. Back closes the frame; the list is at the scroll it had and the chosen row stays highlighted. Opening and closing the pane never moves the list at desktop width: its scroll is the same before, while and after, however its rows wrap when it narrows (lina-app#153). At desktop width the pane's head has `닫기` at its top right (lina-app#161): it and Esc close the pane at once, however many records were opened in it one after another (A, then B from the list: one press closes it, never back to A, lina-app#166), the list keeps the scroll it has, and focus goes back to the row that opened the shown record; Esc typed while a Korean syllable is being composed, or while the bell's list is open, is theirs. A frame opened in 대화 keeps its back control to the agent's card instead. Nothing in the frame is cut, at 390 or 1280 (lina-app#161): a line that would run past it, the card's own name included, wraps at a word instead of ending in `…` (src/detail-qa7.test.tsx, tests/detail-qa7.spec.ts).
- The head reads the title and `진행 중 · 시작 n분 전` (or the stopped, waiting or done line). A stopped card or run reads the same words and the same time as its 중단됨 row on the home, from one function (`stopReason` in `src/model/stop-reason.ts`, the time from `cardTime`): `중단됨 · <owner> 확인 필요 · 3시간 전` on both, never `중단됨 · 결정 필요` on one and `확인 필요` on the other. The head reads the state of the row that was pressed (the list's snapshot) until the next refresh moves both, even when the card's detail, read later, already says another (lina-app#157).
- A stopped card says why (lina-app#157, lina-app#156): the row's stop words follow `중단됨` in the state line (`결정 대기 · <ask>`, `피드백 대기 · <ask>`, `<agent> 검토 대기`, another reason's own word), so no second line repeats them. Only where the state line is `중단됨` alone (a card the core only reads that waits on the owner) or no row is known, one grey line under it says `결정 대기 · <ask>` / `피드백 대기 · <ask>` by the core's `reason`, else the reason's word, else `이유 없음`. When the newest of its comments is the owner's, made after the stop, one more line reads `결정 기록 있음 · 상태는 아직 중단됨`. The owner's `다시 시도해 주세요.` is no decision (lina-app#211): while the card's row reads `<agent>에게 요청함` (actions.md, Retry), one line under the head reads the same words (`requested-since-stop`), and `결정 기록 있음` counts only the owner's other comments; a card with both after the stop has both lines. A request older than the stop reads neither.
- A stopped card that waits on the owner has the place to answer it under its head: a `답 달기` field and `보내기`, sent as the answer to its question (the same as from 수신함). While `보내기` cannot send, one grey line under it says why (`글 입력 대기`, `보내는 중`). On a to-do's or a goal's decision, once `보내기` is pressed and for as long as the toast `답함 · 되돌리기` is up, the head keeps the two lines of the row that was pressed (`목표 › 작업` and the question), the state line reads `답함`, and the answer just sent stands where the question box stood, with no field; `되돌리기` brings the box and the field back, holding the answer that was not sent (lina-app#166). A card stopped on anyone else has none. A work card's head reads the name of the row that was tapped (lina-app#153): the title its row reads (its goal's when the row leads with it), else its checked title, or its own title until the checked one is written — only while that own title passes the app's raw-text rule — shortened to the row width. When the card's own title differs from that name it is the grey line under the head's state line, and the `목표 · <goal>` line shows only when the head does not already read the goal. The kind is never the title (lina-app#149) and shows only when known: the core serves no kind of a card, so a card nothing names a kind for shows no kind, never `코드` (lina-app#153). With no title at all the head reads `제목 없음` in grey. A card opened by its address before the card list is read (a reload on `#/cards/<id>`) reads `불러오는 중` in its head until its row is known, never `제목 없음`, which stays for a card the board does not know once the list is read (lina-app#253). A run outside any card without a checked title reads its row's title the same way.
- A read-only card (`readOnly: true`, every card and goal the core serves from the Hermes board, lina#481; the core refuses its writes with 409) has no write control at all (lina-app#149): no `답 달기`, `보내기`, choice buttons or `다시 시도`, on its detail or its row. No line says it is read-only (G138): with no write control there is nothing to press, so nothing needs saying (the `읽기 전용 · 여기서는 바꿀 수 없음` line of lina-app#153 is gone). Its question still shows as a `결정 필요` box, but its state line makes no claim that the owner must act: only the state word and the time, no `확인 필요` or `피드백 대기`, which show only where an answer box exists. A to-do of the owner's own record stays writable.
- lina-app#171: a stopped card says its state once. The run box has no line of its own repeating `중단됨 · <why>`; the head's state line and the grey line under it are the only place. A reason longer than the row says (the row cuts it at a word to 30 letters with `…`) is said whole under the state line, wrapped, never cut: the words before it (`결정 대기`) on the grey line, then the reason (`stop-reason`). The work inbox's whole reason (`blockReason`), the typed field a bot wrote for the owner, is never cut (lina-app#241): when the one gate below passes it, it shows as written under the state line (`stop-reason`), three lines at most until `더 보기` opens the rest (`접기` closes it); when the gate does not, it is one grey `접힌 이유` line that opens all of it as written. So the question it asks the owner is always in it or one tap away. A decision or feedback stop with no ask and no block reason reads its words alone, `결정 대기` / `피드백 대기`, as its row, never `이유 기록 없음` and never a sentence of a bot's comment (G138). One internal word as `blockReason` (a status name) is no reason. `결정 대기` and `피드백 대기` are said only where the owner's answer is what the card waits for (`waitingOn: owner`, or a question of the card's in the work inbox; a core that does not send `waitingOn` at all is read as before): a decision or feedback stop the core says nobody, or an agent, has to act on (a device to come back) reads `다른 일 대기`, the same word on its row, its head and its stop line (`stopWords`, `waitsOnOwner` in `src/model/index.ts`).
- lina-app#171: a read-only card that waits on the owner (decision, feedback or the owner's check) adds one grey line under its head: `답할 곳 · <agent> 대화`, the agent's name a link to that agent's conversation (`#/talk/<agent>`), drawn in the app's link colour (accent, 4.5:1 on the page and the panel in dark, lina-app#174), not the grey of the line. Only when the card names an agent the app knows; otherwise there is no such line. It is text and a link only: no write is added.
- lina-app#171, #174: a card has one `댓글` section. Its `댓글` box holds the card's comments and the comments between rounds that only its worker runs' record holds, once each, newest first; the card's `기록` has no comment block. A record comment the card also holds (a machine notice the box leaves out too) is never drawn. A worker run's own page has no `댓글` box, so its record's comments between rounds stay `댓글` blocks there.
- lina-app#171: the detail of a folded line (`<title> ×N` in `완료`) lists every card of the line under `묶인 작업 N건`, one `<time> · <state word> · <sentence>` line each, the opened card first, then newest first, each the link to its card.
- An old done card the core keeps no history for (`detail: false` on its list row: no runs or comments in its detail) shows one line, `기록 보관 안 됨`, under its head instead of an empty frame.
- A question the card asks the owner (from the work inbox) shows on top as a `결정 필요` box: the sentence, then agent, `결정 대기` (its 수신함 row's word, lina-app#179) and age.
- Below the boxes and above the comments, `기록` is the run's own timeline (lina#201), on a card (its worker runs' records together) and on a worker run's page. It is the card's one round list: one block per round, newest first by the time its head reads (lina-app#149; its rows newest first too), headed by one history line `<time> · <outcome>[ ×n][ · <report line>]` with no round number (lina-app#61, #123, #142; `어제 23:41` when not today): the time is a going round's start and an ended round's end (its start when the end is not on record), the outcome `진행 중`, `검토 통과`, `검토 필요`, `중단됨`, `완료` or `완료 기록 없음`, then the round's report line when it stands whole by the one rule below and passes the now line's gate (`nowMayShow`: one sentence of 40 letters or fewer, no Latin word, number label, device name, path or hash), else the first sentence of the round's own report line or said rows that passes that gate and says what came of it (`02:11 · 검토 통과 · 병합된 변경 41개를 모음`); never a bot's comment on the card, which is another event and reads in `댓글` (lina-app#269). A going round's head reads the card's top line in that place (`17:24 · 진행 중 · 가짜 목록 셋을 차례로 맞추는 중`, G138), the same as `지금`, and `진행 중` alone when the card has none; a worker run's own page reads its checked now line there. A closed round with no report line that stands whole reads its outcome's one sentence in place of the outcome word (`09:05 · 검토를 통과했다`, `검토를 통과하지 못했다`, `작업이 중단됐다`, `작업을 마쳤다`, `완료 기록이 없다`), so no line of `기록` is a time and a status word alone. Rounds of one outcome with nothing else in them, one after another, are one head at the newest one's time with `×n` (`16:41 · 검토를 통과했다 ×3`, lina-app#236), the link to the newest one's worker run; a head with a sentence or another outcome is never in the count, and the comments a card draws in its `댓글` box do not keep two such rounds apart (lina-app#269). There is no second result line. On a card each round's head is the keyboard-reachable link to that round's worker run (`#/runs/<id>`). Rows read oldest first, one event per line in the form `<time> · <one sentence>` (`14:05`, `어제 23:41`, `11월 9일 08:10`; lina-app#123, #128): the run's sentences in the label colour, the owner's comments with author and time, a sentence said n times in a row once with `×n`; a round whose start is not on record opens with one dim `시작 기록 없음`; a round's result line follows its rows (`결과`); a comment made between rounds is its own block. A sentence or a result line that would show raw English, a path, a command, an id or a run number is left out, with no placeholder; a round left with no rows still shows its head. Tool steps are never a line of their own (G138): beside what a going card does now they are left out, and otherwise each `도구 사용 n번` is held in the one `접힌 기록` line, which opens to it. A comment or row that would show raw text stays as a folded entry (`접힌 댓글`, `접힌 기록`): every one of them stands once, as a single `<time> · 접힌 기록 n건` line after the last block, never a line between two rounds. Each block folds; its open state and the scroll survive the record's refetch on its run's `worker` change event. A failed read shows one line `읽기 실패: 기록` in its place, and the rest of the detail keeps working.
- A card's detail is read when the card opens, never at the first load (lina-app#100). While it is read the card shows its list row at once: the head (title, kind, state and time) and `지금`, with `불러오는 중` below; the boxes fill in when it answers. The history (`기록`) and `댓글` are drawn once (lina-app#217): the records of its worker runs are read only after the detail names them, so until they have answered, that one `불러오는 중` line stays in their place, never the worker runs' box drawn from the detail alone (`이전 기록 N건`) that the records then redraw longer. The record of the worker run seen last is read first and alone (lina-app#232): on the hub's long card each of 110 runs serves the card's whole record (about 300 kB), and waiting for all of them kept a phone at `불러오는 중` for about 20 s. The other records are read then, newest run first and at most four at once, never while a list (cards, board, goals, items, inbox) is being read, so a list the open screen needs never waits behind a long card's records (lina-app#253: the hub's biggest card names 142 worker runs, its core answers one read after another, and asked all at once they held the card list for 8.6 s and the history for 12.9 s); a record a screen opens meanwhile (a worker run's page) is read before the card's others. `기록` draws as soon as the records in so far, newest first up to the first one still out, hold a round (the newest run's record may hold none, as a browser run's), with the one `불러오는 중` line under it; each later record is an older run's and adds below, so the first history row neither moves nor changes, and `댓글` (which takes the records' comments too) comes when they all have. A record that lands out of that order (an event named it) is drawn once the newer ones are in. Once drawn, a record a new worker run brings adds to them in place; opened again, the card draws them at once.
- A worker run a card started is not on the board's list (the core lists only runs outside any card); its round's head still opens its own page: the card it belongs to and its timeline. Opened on its own before its card (a reload or a link from elsewhere), the run's record names its card (`cardId`) and the page reads that card's detail: meanwhile it shows the kind word, model line and `불러오는 중`, never a round outcome it cannot know yet; then the card row and the timeline as when opened from the card. A worker run with no record to read (an older run the core has none for) cannot name its card before the card opens: its page keeps the kind word and one `읽기 실패: 기록` line, never `찾을 수 없음`. The same one line stands when such a run's page is opened from its card's 실행 line or by its card's run address, once the card is read (lina-app#110). A worker run's state line reads that run's own end, never its card's state (lina-app#61): its record's newest round, else the round of the card it was (`완료` for a round that ended, `중단됨` for one with no end that no longer beats, `결과 누락` when no round holds it), `진행 중` only while that round goes.
- **A card's top line (G138).** A card at work or waiting has at most one short top line, from one function (`cardNow` in `src/model/index.ts`), which its `지금` box, its going round's head in `기록` (`<time> · 진행 중 · <top line>`) and, on a wide window, its row's second line all read, so they never say two things. It is (a) the core's checked now line (`plain.now`) when it is one sentence of at most 40 letters on one line that the one gate passes, with no card id, worker id, issue number, path, command or English word (`nowMayShow` in `src/model/fold.ts`; a longer or other line is no now line, whatever the core's own 60-letter cap); else (b) a short line the app makes from the state it already has, only as far as the core says: a card whose answer the owner owes (`waitingOn: owner`, or a question of the card's in the work inbox) reads its wait word, `결정 대기` or `피드백 대기` (`확인 필요` with no reason); a wait on another to-do reads `다른 일을 기다리는 중`, on another agent `다른 에이전트를 기다리는 중`, on a review `검토를 기다리는 중`, on a gate `검사를 기다리는 중`, on a set time `정한 시각을 기다리는 중`; else (c) nothing: `지금` is not drawn and the going round's head reads `진행 중` alone. What the card does is never guessed: no bot comment, no sentence of one, no block reason and no ask is ever its top line; its comments stay in `댓글`, folded when they do not stand whole, and open as written. A stopped card's wait is said by its head, so its top line is only the core's now line, and none when the head already says the same words.
- An English worker hand-out line a bot writes on a card (no Hangul, `assigned`, `dispatched`, `claimed`, `spawned` or `handed off/over` with a worker, run or task: `Assigned worker w-4601 to run 1 (lane A)`) is a machine notice, as `[받음]` is (G138): no line, and no count in `접힌 댓글`. It is never turned into a Korean sentence.
- `할 일` lists the to-dos the card covers, each with a round mark that only shows its state: the card cannot change a to-do, so the mark is no control and has no action name (no `<title> 완료로 표시`); a done one reads `완료` to a screen reader (lina-app#128).
- The worker runs' box is headed `기록`, the run history's one name whether the card runs or stopped (lina-app#179). It stands only while the record is not shown (no record yet, or with no head of its own under the `기록` box's error line when its read failed, so the worker links never disappear). It lists the worker runs the card started, one line each, `<time> · <state word>`: newest first (lina-app#157): when it started (first seen), then `진행 중` while it beats, else how its round ended (`완료`, `중단됨`), else `결과 누락` (lina-app#166, the screen words table: an ended run with no result); never `코드 작업`, and never the card's title again on every line (the head carries it, lina-app#142). They never show as their own card elsewhere. Each run of rounds whose own line may not show is one folded row `이전 기록 N건`, their heads and worker runs inside, never one `이전 기록 1건` row per round (lina-app#161). A stopped card says why above that box, one grey line `중단됨 · <reason word>` (`결정 대기`, `피드백 대기`, another reason's own word, else `이유 없음`), since its rounds may all read `완료` (lina-app#161).
- A to-do the card covers is checked only when it is done; a card whose run is still going covers open to-dos.
- `댓글` shows each comment as one history line, `<time> · <comment>`, newest first like every history (lina-app#149); the author is the line's tooltip. **The owner's own comment is drawn exactly as saved** (lina-app#226), on a card's `댓글` lines, a to-do's or a goal's thread and its `기록`: the same characters, markdown marks and ids included, only the row's own width folding a long one. Whose a comment is comes from its author alone (`owner`, or the owner's id), never from its words: a comment by another agent, a profile name or no author on the owner's card goes through the one rule below (lina-app#230). A worker run's record comment row has no author (the core makes it from a receipt's note, a verdict note or a cancel reason, which on the hub an agent writes, joined to one line and cut at 300 characters with no mark): in `댓글` and in a round of `기록` it reads through the same rule, and a cut one is longer than a whole line may be, so it folds (lina-app#236); a row that is the core's cut of a comment the card holds is not drawn again. A bot's comment is drawn whole or folded, by the one rule below. No markdown emphasis marker (`**`, `*`, `~~`, `__`) of a bot's text reaches a history line, a round's head, a comment or a question: the marker goes and the words stay, and an entry that is only markers and spaces is no row at all (`plainText` in `src/model/plain.ts`, lina-app#166). A machine notice (by `kanban-dispatcher` or `lina-worker`, or opening with a tag of words alone such as `[받음]`, `[대기 충족]`, `[현황]`) is no line. The bracketed stamp that opens a comment (`[<role> 02:21 결정]`, who wrote it and when) is no word of it. A comment that is only a state word (`완료`) reads as that word, and state words that follow each other are one line at the newest time with `×n` (lina-app#153, #236). A run of comments not drawn is one `접힌 댓글` line (`접힌 댓글 n건`). A comment drawn on more than one line of a card is one line at its newest time with `×n` (lina-app#230); a state word alone is no sentence and is not counted. A comment row in `기록` is the same one line, and comments made one after another between rounds are one `댓글` block.
- **One rule draws every bot text** (lina-app#236, #241; `unitMayShow` and `wholeText` in `src/model/fold.ts`): a text whose author is not the owner is one unit, on a card's `댓글` and `기록` (its comment and said rows and its round heads' report lines), a thread, a goal's merge lines and question, and a stop's reason. The one gate decides whether the unit may be drawn as its own text: no Latin letter, internal name, ref, id or hash, no mark that needs text after it, no cut-off end (below). There is no per-sentence drawing, no list unit, no lead-in and no continuation rule: a unit is drawn **whole or folded**, never a part of it, and an event of a known kind still reads as its kind word (a round's outcome, `질문`, `완료`). The only things taken out are its markdown markers, the stamp it opens with and a bracket that holds nothing but refs, ids or hashes (`(lina#12)`, `(#123·#124)`, `(t_ab12cd34)`, `(a1b2c3d)`), with the space before it.
- **Folded is not gone** (lina-app#241): every folded line (`<time> · 접힌 댓글 n건` on a card and a goal, `접힌 댓글 n건` in a thread, `<time> · 접힌 기록 n건` in `기록`, `접힌 이유` under a stop) is a button (`aria-expanded`) that opens the texts it holds, each whole and as written with its lines, in the order of the history around it, each under its time and its author's display name (none for a record row or an author the app has no name for, never an id). The gate does not read an opened text: the owner chose to read it. Machine notices stay out. `접기` at its end closes it and puts focus back on the line; opening never moves the detail's scroll, at 390 or 1280.
- **The limit** of a closed line: a unit is one closed line only when it is one line of at most **2 sentences and 120 letters** (a number's `2.` or `0.` ends no sentence) and the gate passes it; else it folds, and opens whole. Measured on the hub's long card (lina-app#241, 581 comments): its 231 bot comments that are no machine notice are 665 letters at the median and 1265 at the 90th percentile, and every one holds a ref, an id or a Latin word, so all of them fold whatever the limit; what the fold changes is that it opens. An ended round's said row is the tail the core kept of a longer text (it opens or ends anywhere in it, or joins events with ` / `): no unit, so it always folds into `접힌 기록 n건`; a going round's said rows go through the gate. A report line that does not pass folds there too, so a round of one outcome with nothing drawn still counts on its neighbour's head (`×n`).
- A sentence of it does **not read** when it holds a path, a file name, a command, a code name (`a_b`, `aB`, `a=b`), a code-like token (`G2`, `S2`, `rc 0`), any Latin letter or a ref outside such a bracket (`#12`, an id, a hash, a hash of digits alone: a run of seven digits or more such as `4815162342`, lina-app#228, unless a unit is glued after it, `12345678원`, or a thousands comma, which make it a number, lina-app#230); an arrow or a slash (`3 → 5`, `앱/코어`); the hub's own cut (`…`); a time of day that reads as a log's (lina-app#153, #230): two or more in it, one with seconds, or one with no Korean particle or word glued after it (`12:50 · 시작`, `… 12:50`, `12:50.`, `22:45 기준으로`, a pasted log line's head). **Exactly one time with a particle or word glued after it (`12:50에`, `9:05부터`, `14:00까지`, `12:50경`, `12:50쯤`) reads** when the sentence otherwise does: it is a plain fact, and the row's own time beside it is a different time the two read side by side (`13:02 · 영수증 화면은 12:50에 다시 올렸다.`). Why glued only: the issue's examples are all glued, and a log line opens on a time and a space; the owner may widen it (lina-app#230 decision, reversible); or no Hangul. It does not read either when it starts inside another sentence (a separator, a closing quote, a particle alone, a range's tail `~26초`, `.5초`) or ends open (`:`, `|`, an opening bracket, a quote left open, a list's first mark `순서는 1.`), or when a sentence of it is cut off before its verb: it ends on the particle of an object, a place, a subject or a topic (`… 세부를`, `고치는 중이`, `작업은`), with or without an end mark (never `가`, which ends many nouns). A short unit with no predicate (`원인 둘.`) and one that opens on `그러나` are still whole units and are drawn when they pass.
- One rule orders every history: by time, newest first (lina-app#153), the worker runs under a round too (lina-app#157). Every round whose own line may not show is gathered into one `이전 기록 N건` row after the last round (lina-app#161), never a row between two rounds: a folded block is named `댓글` only when it holds comments. A card's worker runs that serve the same record show each round and each comment once, and a round with nothing in it but the outcome of the round before it is counted on that round's head (`×n`, lina-app#236).
- A run outside any card opens at `#/runs/<id>` with its own state and the to-do it takes on.
- A card's worker run's page heads itself with its card's name as the card's row reads it (lina-app#248): its goal's title first (lina-app#122), else the card's own title, else its kind word, grey; so a card whose own title no rule draws (a Latin word in it) is named on its run page as on its row and its head. A to-do's run line for that run reads the same name once the card is known. `제목 없음` stays only for a run whose card the board does not know, and for a run with no record whose card nothing names yet.
- A card's worker run opens at `#/cards/<card>/runs/<n>`, `n` its place among the card's worker runs from 1: the round heads and the worker run lines link there, so no address on a card holds the run's own id, a task id no person reads that shows on hover and in the status bar (lina-app#238). The address alone opens the same page, after a reload too.
- A 기록 block's head opens the worker run whose record holds that block (lina-app#245): on the hub each worker run serves its own record, which numbers its rounds from 1, so a block never opens the card's first run for its number alone, and both rounds of a run with two link that run. Only a record that holds rounds of the card's other runs (one run serving the card's one record, lina-app#153) links each such round to the run of the card round with its number and time.
- A worker run's page names the model that run used once, under its head, by the plain name the model list gives it, with its effort (`<name> · 보통`). A run that started on the fallback model ends the line with `대체 모델` (`<name> · 높음 · 대체 모델`). A model the list names only by an id is an alias (`model-a`, `model-b`) and draws no line (lina-app#128); neither does a run with no model. The rounds of that page's 기록 draw no model line. The settings revision is not shown.
- On a card's 기록, each round shows its own run's model under the round's head, dim, once: the worker run that round was, the same one its head opens. A round whose run has none shows nothing. A comment block shows none.
- Only boxes that add something are drawn.
- Opened on a running, waiting or stopped card of the hub's real data, a card's top line alone tells within ten seconds what the card is doing now (G138, lina-app#263): three or more cards of each state, read at 1280 and 390; a card whose top line cannot say it is a defect, whatever its shape.
- A card's `기록` line says `검토 통과` or `검토 필요` only for a round's verdict, and the sentence after it is that round's own Hangul sentence of 40 letters or fewer, or none (lina-app#269). Read it on a hub real card from the top line of `기록` down: if any one line reads a progress comment as a review result, it is a defect.
- A stopped card's `결정 대기` or `피드백 대기` stands only on a card that waits on the owner's answer (G138): the core says so (`waitingOn: owner`) or the card's question is in the work inbox. The hub sends no `waitingOn` on its own cards (`hermes:<id>`), so a decision or feedback stop of one with no question in the work inbox (a device to come back, another run to end) reads `중단됨 · 다른 일 대기` on its row, its detail head and its stop line alike, and is not put first among the stops. A card of any other id with no `waitingOn` at all is read as before.

## Sub-features

- `card-head` shows the name of the row that was tapped (never a kind word), the card's own title under it when it differs, the kind only when known, the row's state and time, why a stopped card stopped, and `답할 곳 · <agent> 대화` on a read-only card that waits on the owner
- `card-now` shows the card's top line, the one its going round's head and its row read (G138)
- `card-runs` dims the worker runs it started
- `run-detail` opens a run outside any card, or a card's own worker run
- `run-model` names a run's model once under its head; a card's 기록 also names each round's own run
- `card-timeline` shows the run's own record round by round, one `<time> · <sentence>` line per event; a finished run with no record lists its start and its result line

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose a card on 진행 중인 작업, 완료, an agent screen or a goal page.
- Choose a `결정 필요` row in 수신함 whose small line reads `목표 › 작업`: it is the card's question; choosing it opens the card in the detail frame with the question box on top and the place to answer it.
- Choose a 검토 필요 row in 수신함 for a run.
