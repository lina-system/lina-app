# 대화 tab

The first of the three tabs at the top (`대화 · 작업 · 상태`). The owner chooses one agent and talks with it in the middle, in that agent's own app conversation (lina#482), with the agent's card, its goals, beside the conversation. There is no conversation with all agents at once.

## Platforms

- `web`: shipped.
- `mac`: planned (M1).
- `iphone`: planned (M1).

## Intended behavior

- The tabs `대화`, `작업`, `상태` sit in the toolbar on a wide window and under the bar on a phone's first screen of each tab. The tab of the screen shown is pressed, and it is that screen's one title: no large title over it on a phone, and no title beside it in the wide toolbar on `#/talk` with no agent, `#/` and `#/status`; a list with a name of its own (`수신함`, `마감일`, …) keeps it in the toolbar (lina-app#193, `src/tab-root-head.test.tsx`). `대화` opens `#/talk`, `작업` opens `#/`, `상태` opens `#/status`.
- The pressed tab is one look at every width: a segment in `--tab-current` on the `--tab-track` the other tabs show. In dark it stands at least 3:1 against its neighbours, on a phone too (lina-app#143, `src/design/contrast.test.ts`).
- Every text on the first screens of the three tabs (`#/talk`, `#/`, `#/status`) reaches 4.5:1 against what it is drawn on, 3:1 when large (24 px, or 18.66 px bold), at 390 and 1280, light and dark, measured from computed styles (lina-app#213). White words on red (the bell's count, the stopped pills) sit on `--red-fill`, the state red's hue at 4.78:1 (`src/design/contrast.test.ts`). The pairs only a palette change clears (white on the accent fill, the accent as a text button's colour in light, the state red and orange as words) are listed as open in `scripts/verify.mjs`; any other pair under its ratio is a defect.
- At 390 the list's bar reads like a chat app's list head: at its left an icon of three lines named `메뉴`, with no word, the same entry the 작업 and 상태 tabs' first screens have, opening the same sheet (lina-app#167, #193); then the install chip and the bell.
- `#/talk` lists the agents. On a wide window they are the sidebar and the middle, the whole width with no empty right column, reads `에이전트를 고르면 대화가 열림` (lina-app#150); on a phone they are the screen's rows. Every row, at every width, has the first line of the agent's last message under its name (one line; a reply's markdown marks such as `##`, `>`, `-`, `**`, backticks and a link's address are left out, lina-app#187), or `아직 나눈 대화 없음` when there is none, and the same counts at the right of the name's line, in words as the row's name reads them (`<name> · 진행 중 1 · 중단됨 6`): the work in progress in grey, `진행 중 n`, then the stopped work in red, `중단됨 n` (lina-app#143); a bare number beside `아직 나눈 대화 없음` read as unread messages (lina-app#207, #219, `src/screens.test.tsx`, `src/talk-rows.test.tsx`). The last message's line is the row's whole width, counts or none, and ends in `…` only when the message is longer than that (lina-app#219, `tests/talk-tab.spec.ts`); a long name gives way to the counts. While a turn to that agent goes, its row reads `답하는 중` instead, at every width (lina-app#190): not the owner's own message and not what the core holds mid-turn; when the reply ends the row reads the reply's first line, when it breaks off the owner's message (`src/talk-preview.test.tsx`).
- The rows read their last messages at most 2 at a time (lina-app#162): a browser keeps 6 connections to one host and a gateway that does not answer holds each read about 30 s, so the live hub's 9 rows reading at once left none for the change stream and the store, and the head read `갱신 지연` on first entry until they gave up. The open window's own read does not wait behind them (`src/talk-preview.test.tsx`).
- Choosing an agent opens `#/talk/<agent>`: the earlier turns of that app conversation read from the core (`GET /api/lina/agents/<agent>/talk`, the core's `[LINA app]` first line and the blank line after it left out, so no empty line stands above the owner's message, lina-app#150), the field `메시지` and `보내기`. With no earlier turn it reads `아직 나눈 대화 없음`.
- The window's head is a chat app's head (lina-app#191): the agent's mark and its name (`person.name` from the people list, as the core gives it) are the screen's one title, in the bar, never in the scrolling window, so they stay while the conversation scrolls. On a phone the bar reads, left to right, a back icon with no word (named `대화`), the mark and name, then the install chip and the bell; a hairline under the bar marks its edge over the window, and the top safe area stays above it. The bar is 58 px tall above the safe area, the height of every phone bar, the tab roots' too, so switching tabs moves nothing; the picture is 38 px with 10 px above and below it (36 px in the wide toolbar, lina-app#206), and the name is 20 px, one step over the 17 px body (lina-app#202, `tests/phone-head.spec.ts` at 390 light and dark). On a wide window the mark and name stand once in the toolbar, not again over the window. No help line stands under the name.
- An agent's mark is its picture when the people list says `avatar: true` (`GET /api/lina/agents/<agent>/avatar`, same origin, lina#738), everywhere the mark shows (the rows, the bar, each agent group in the window, the agent's screen, work rows), at the size of the circle. Otherwise, and when the picture fails to load (404, offline), it is the coloured circle with the first letter: no broken image, no jump. The six circle colours (`--agent-0`..`--agent-5`, the same in light and dark) hold the white letter at 4.5:1 or more and none reads as a state colour: each stays 30 or more CIE76 units and 30 or more degrees of OKLCH hue from the accent, red, orange and the machines' green (lina-app#190, #200; `src/design/contrast.test.ts`).
- While the earlier turns are being read the window reads `불러오는 중`, not an empty box (a gateway that does not answer holds that read about 30 s, lina-app#154).
- An address naming no agent the core lists (`#/talk/<unknown>`) reads `찾을 수 없는 대화` with a link `대화` back to the list: no field, no send, no raw id (lina-app#154).
- Enter or `보내기` sends (`POST /api/lina/agents/<agent>/talk`) at once, never held behind the history read (lina-app#154). The field empties, the message shows on the right at once, drawn fainter until the core answers the send, the reply streams in on the left with three typing dots in its bubble (still when motion is reduced), and stays when it ends. A reopened window shows the exchange again.
- A turn sent from another device (the phone, another window) shows here with no reload (lina-app#205): once its stream is over the core's change stream sends a `talk` event naming the agent, and that agent's row reads its last message again and its open window its earlier turns, which then hold the other device's message and reply, each exchange once. While a turn of this page's goes, the window waits for that turn's own event. Another agent's row and window read nothing (see [Live updates](./live.md)).
- The window reads like a phone's messages app (lina-app#187), at 390 and 1280, light and dark: the owner's messages are rounded bubbles on the right in the accent, plain text as typed; the agent's on the left, under its mark (its picture or the coloured circle with its first letter, `AgentMark`) and its name, once at the top of each group. Consecutive messages of one sender within 5 minutes are one group with a small gap, its last bubble with a tighter corner and its time (`14:05`, small and grey) once at its end. A date stands before each day's first message: `오늘`, `어제`, else `11월 13일` (with the year when it is not this year). A bubble is at most 75% of the column (and 640 px).
- The agent's reply draws its markdown (`src/markdown.tsx`, rules in `src/markdown.test.tsx`): bold, italic, inline code, fenced code blocks (monospace, own background, a long line scrolls sideways inside the block), bullet and numbered lists, links (http, https and mailto only, a new tab, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`), blockquotes, headings as bold text, and tables (scrolling sideways inside the bubble). Nothing a reply writes as HTML is ever an element; a link to any other scheme and an image stay as written. A mark with no end yet (while the reply streams) is text until it closes.
- A new message, the owner's or the agent's, scrolls the window to the bottom. Read up (more than 48 px from the end), a new message does not pull the owner down: a round `맨 아래로` button over the bottom of the thread shows, with the count of messages that came in meanwhile (`맨 아래로 1`), and returns to the bottom. The owner's own message always goes to the bottom.
- The field is rounded and grows with its lines up to 5, then scrolls; Enter sends, Shift+Enter breaks a line, and an Enter that ends a Hangul composition only finishes the syllable. `보내기` is a round icon button, its name `보내기`, in the accent when there is something to send and dim while the field is empty or a turn is going; while a turn is going a short grey word beside it on the field's row says why, in the words `댓글` and `답` use (lina-app#200; lina-app#158): `보내는 중` while the message goes out, `답하는 중` while the reply streams; an empty field says nothing (the owner took `글 입력 대기` out of the chat, 11-15); never among the turns, and the bar keeps its height, so the field stays where `tests/bottom-strip.spec.ts` and `tests/talk-tab.spec.ts` hold it.
- Leaving the window while the reply streams (another agent, the list, another tab) does not cancel the turn: the request runs to its end (lina-app#127). Coming back, the sent message is in the agent's window, the reply streams on under it and stays, or `답을 받지 못함` shows if it broke off; `보내기` stays disabled until it ends. Why not cancel: the owner has no way to stop a turn (see Not goals), so leaving is not a cancel, and whether a cut request cancels the agent's turn on the core is not confirmed, so a cut request could leave a turn the window no longer shows.
- The agent's card: on a wide window the right column headed `에이전트` (never `카드`; the counts, the agent's goals, `완료 · 오늘 n`); on a phone the same card folded above the conversation as `에이전트 · 목표 n` with each goal's progress, 10 px under the bar's edge so the two never touch (lina-app#202), opening to the same rows; it stays pinned there while a conversation of many screens scrolls under it, at the end, the middle and the top alike, on the window's grey so no message shows between the bar and the strip: the bar never moves, so the strip does not slide away and back either (lina-app#206, `tests/layout-qa15.spec.ts`); open, that line goes, since the rows say the same (lina-app#190, `src/talk-card.test.tsx`). Each goal row writes its progress `<done>/<total>` as text beside the dial at every width (lina-app#175); every `n/n` goal, whatever n, draws the done mark (the checked, struck-through check) instead of the dial, also one that still waits to be closed, which its page closes (lina-app#200), and a goal with an open to-do keeps its dial (`src/talk-card.test.tsx`), and that number is the goal's own: done and all of the to-dos its page lists, every agent's included (`goalTodos`, the one count the folded band, the goal lists and the agent screen read), never only this agent's. A goal with no to-dos shows its name alone there, never `0/0`, as on its row (lina-app#160, #167). That summary is one line by design (cut, every goal a tap away), like the agent row's last message, the tabs and the chips; nowhere else on this screen is text cut: a goal's title and its done condition wrap at a word in the 320 px right column at 1280 as on the phone (lina-app#156, #167). The counts read `진행 중 n · 중단됨 n · 검토 필요 n · 목표 n`: every number after its word, `중단됨 n` only when not 0 and the only red part.
- A goal chosen on the card stays under `대화` (`#/talk/<agent>?goal=<id>`, lina-app#143). On a wide window it opens in the right column beside the window, with back `에이전트` to the card; on a phone it is one screen deeper, with back reading the agent's name. It is the same goal page as from `작업`: its state, its to-dos' states and its `작업` rows are the work tab's fold (goals.md, lina-app#170), so a goal whose cards are all stopped reads `중단됨` here too and lists those cards. The goal's own links (its to-dos) open under `작업` as before.
- On a phone, with all well, no bottom strip is drawn and the field bar sits at the bottom of the screen, the field 10 px above the home indicator or the keyboard, like a chat app's; with a word in the strip the field sits 10 px above the strip (lina-app#187, `tests/bottom-strip.spec.ts` at 390 light and dark, with and without a 300 px keyboard).
- On a phone, with the card open, a goal row scrolled into view stops above the field and the bottom strip (the screen's scroll padding keeps their height clear).
- On a phone the open card (its counts, goals and `완료 · 오늘 n`) scrolls in its own region, which ends above the field and the bottom strip with the window's head and the card's toggle in view: with 28 goals the last one, scrolled to inside the region, is the element at its own centre (lina-app#162).
- A refused turn shows one red line as soon as the core answers, the message leaves the window and comes back into the field (unless something new was typed meanwhile). The line reads the error code in the body before the status (lina-app#158): `에이전트가 바쁨` (429, and 409 without `no_owning_agent`: the agent gateway's `admission_conflict` and others), `맡은 에이전트 없음` (409 `no_owning_agent`, the core's own, only in a to-do's `대화`), `에이전트 연결 안 됨` (503), `없는 에이전트` (404), `에이전트가 연결을 거절함` (502), `글은 4000자까지` (413), any other `저장하지 못함 · 다시 시도`. A reply that breaks off reads `답을 받지 못함`.
- The line sits inside the field's bar, above the field: on a phone the bar is sticky over the bottom of the screen and nothing covers the line, with or without the keyboard's height (lina-app#158).
- On a phone the field's bar is opaque (`--bg`): a message scrolled under it never shows through the field, with the card's goals open too (lina-app#167).

## Sub-features

- `tabs` shows the three tabs and opens each tab's first screen
- `talk-agents` lists the agents to talk with, and no window on all of them
- `talk-history` shows the earlier turns of the agent's app conversation
- `talk-agent-send` sends and streams the reply in the agent's own window
- `talk-card` shows the agent's card and goals beside the window, folded on a phone
- `talk-goal` opens a goal from the card under `대화` and back returns to the window
- `talk-contrast` holds every text on the three tabs' first screens at 4.5:1 (3:1 large), light and dark

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose `대화` in the tabs, then an agent.
- Address `/app/#/talk`, `/app/#/talk/<agent>` or `/app/#/talk/<agent>?goal=<id>`.

## Gotchas

- `보내기` also exists in `댓글` and in a to-do's `대화`; find this one inside `getByTestId("agent-talk")`.
- On a live core a turn runs on the agent's gateway; a read-only pass never sends one.
- A spec that serves the core with `page.route` blocks service workers (`test.use({ serviceWorkers: "block" })`): once the app's worker takes the page its reads go past `page.route` to `vite preview`, which answers 404.
- The synthetic window keeps its turns in the page's copy of the scene only, shared by the sources on it like one gateway: a reload starts the scene again. Another device's turn is the live drive's `&live=talk` step.
- The synthetic source keeps a turn in the agent's earlier turns only when its reply completes; until then a reopened window shows it from the page's own record of what it sent.

## Not goals

- No conversation with all agents at once.
- No messenger chat: this window is the agent's app conversation only.
- No delivery state per message (lina-system/lina-app#72), no stopping a reply mid-stream.
